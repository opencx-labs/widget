Add `theme.colorScheme` (`light` | `dark` | `system`). `dark` renders the chosen palette from its dark end across every surface, including the companion shell and the docked sidebar canvas; `system` follows the visitor's OS preference live. The default stays `light`, so existing embeds are unchanged.
